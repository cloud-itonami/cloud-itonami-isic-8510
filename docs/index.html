The Offer

SchoolOps-LLM ⊣ Curriculum Safeguarding Governor: an open-source business blueprint that any qualified, licensed school operator can fork, deploy, and run. This actor covers student enrollment intake, jurisdiction assessment, staff background-check screening, progress-report and promotion proposals, and immutable audit ledgers.

Built on the langgraph-clj StateGraph runtime with an independent Curriculum Safeguarding Governor that enforces hard gates: a fabricated jurisdiction citation, incomplete evidence, a destination class size exceeding its recorded maximum, or an uncleared staff background check each force a hold, not an override.

Every promotion or safeguarding-relevant record is finalized only with human sign-off from a licensed educator, via an approval workflow with a complete audit ledger trail. Designed for schools that need governed, spec-cited, audited execution without building the compliance layer from scratch.
